# Ethics in Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity professionals are responsible for protecting systems, data, and users. Ethical behavior is essential to maintain trust and ensure responsible use of skills.

---

## Key Principles

### Confidentiality
- Protect sensitive information from unauthorized access
- Do not disclose data without permission

### Integrity
- Ensure data is accurate and not altered improperly
- Avoid manipulating systems or information unethically

### Availability
- Help maintain system uptime and accessibility
- Avoid actions that disrupt services

---

## Ethical Responsibilities

- Follow laws and regulations
- Respect user privacy
- Act in the best interest of the organization and users
- Report security issues responsibly
- Avoid conflicts of interest

---

## Common Ethical Issues

- Unauthorized access to systems
- Misuse of privileged access
- Data privacy violations
- Social engineering or manipulation

---

## Responsible Disclosure

- Report vulnerabilities to the appropriate organization
- Do not exploit vulnerabilities for personal gain
- Allow time for fixes before public disclosure

---

## Why Ethics Matter

Ethical behavior in cybersecurity:
- Builds trust with organizations and users
- Prevents legal consequences
- Supports a strong security culture
- Ensures responsible use of technical skills
