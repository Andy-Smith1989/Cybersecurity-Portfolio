# NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) Functions

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) provides guidance for managing and reducing cybersecurity risk through six core functions.

---

## 1. Govern
Establishes organizational cybersecurity risk management strategy, expectations, and policies.

### Key Activities
- Define cybersecurity roles and responsibilities
- Establish governance policies
- Manage cybersecurity strategy

---

## 2. Identify
Develop an understanding of organizational systems, assets, data, and risks.

### Key Activities
- Inventory assets
- Assess risks
- Understand business environment
- Identify vulnerabilities

---

## 3. Protect
Implement safeguards to ensure delivery of critical services.

### Key Activities
- Access control
- Security awareness training
- Data security
- Protective technologies

---

## 4. Detect
Develop and implement activities to identify cybersecurity events.

### Key Activities
- Continuous monitoring
- Security event detection
- Anomaly identification

---

## 5. Respond
Take action regarding detected cybersecurity incidents.

### Key Activities
- Incident response planning
- Communications
- Analysis and mitigation

---

## 6. Recover
Restore capabilities and services after an incident.

### Key Activities
- Recovery planning
- Improvements after incidents
- Communications during recovery

---

## Why These Functions Matter

The NIST CSF helps organizations:
- Build mature cybersecurity programs
- Improve incident preparedness
- Organize security responsibilities
- Strengthen resilience against threats
