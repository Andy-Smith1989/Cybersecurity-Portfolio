# Phishing Email Analysis

## Objective
To determine whether an email is a phishing attempt by analyzing common indicators.

## Indicators Found
- Suspicious sender email address
- Mismatched or hidden URLs
- Urgent or threatening language
- Request for sensitive information

## Tools Used
- Manual inspection

## Conclusion
The email contains multiple indicators commonly associated with phishing attacks and is likely malicious.
